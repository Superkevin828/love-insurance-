Questions people ask us

Yes, we've heard them all.

Does a one-sided crush count?

If you were truly in love and they never knew, we respect the pain. A crush claim gets the ice cream and the best friend session, but not the hotel. We have to stay in business.

How do I prove my heart is broken?

Our Certified Heartbreak Assessors accept a sad playlist, a long text you didn't send, or a sigh of at least four seconds. Dramatic eye rolls are scored extra.

Can I claim for a breakup I caused?

Yes. Dumping someone is sad too. We still cover you, but the best friend may ask questions.

What if I fall back in love with my ex?

Your cover keeps going. We'll gently suggest a second best friend session first, just to be safe.

How soon can I make a claim?

Straight after registration. We believe heartbreak doesn't wait for paperwork.
